What it would take
The shelf holds testimony and poetry — the voice of the thing. It holds almost no history: no account of 1948, no legal or political scholarship, no economic history of the occupation.
A six-week course would need roughly four imported readings — two thirds of the syllabus. At that point the library isn't teaching; it's borrowing.
